Our Story

Makeup is a way
to be yourself.

Not a way to become someone else.

atelierR began with a makeup chair, not a shop. Working with clients in Singapore, the same questions came up again and again — which foundation, which shade, why does this separate by lunchtime, what do I actually need.

The answers were always a small number of products. Not the newest, not the most advertised: the ones that behaved well on real faces, in this climate, in ordinary light. Clients started asking where to buy them. This shop is that answer, written down.

Curated through professional practice.

Only the products atelierR genuinely uses, trusts, and recommends.

How we choose

  1. It has to work on a client, in a session, before it is stocked here.
  2. It has to hold up in Singapore humidity — not in a Tokyo winter photograph.
  3. It has to be forgiving enough that someone can use it well at home, alone, in a hurry.
  4. If we stop recommending it, we stop stocking it.

Why the site works this way

A shop should not require a conversation. Every product page here tells you what it is, why we selected it, who it suits, how to use it, how to choose a shade and whether it is in stock — before you ask.

Payment is by PayNow, checked by a person. It is not the most automated way to run a shop, but it is honest: nothing is ever marked as paid until we have actually seen the money arrive.
